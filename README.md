# Realm of Regions 2

**A browser RPG designed and created by Simon Iacopelli — sequel to Realm of Regions.**

This is a standalone game and is not part of the AppADay portfolio.

-----

## How to Play

1. Enter your name and begin your adventure.
1. Travel through five regions, unlocked in order: **Forest**, **Mountain**, **Lava**, **Frost**, and **Shadow**.
1. Fight enemies to earn XP and gold, level up, and grow stronger.
1. Visit the **Item Store** to buy potions, weapons, armor, magic books, and bombs.
1. Challenge each region’s Demon boss to unlock the next region and claim their heart.
1. Defeat the **Shadow Demon** — the final boss — to complete the game.

The game **autosaves** after every battle, purchase, and item use. Closing and reopening the game picks up right where you left off. A “New Game” link on the World Map lets you wipe your save and start over.

-----

## Battle Actions

- **Attack** — basic weapon strike
- **Magic** — costs 5 MP, hits harder
- **Defend** — block all damage and reflect 10 damage back (limited to 10 uses per battle)
- **Item** — use a Potion, Pancake, or throw a Bomb for bonus damage
- **Act** — talk to the enemy, making them miss their next turn
- **Spare** — after acting, spare a regular enemy to end the battle peacefully (no rewards), or attempt to spare a boss at your own risk — bosses are enraged by mercy and strike back hard
- **Heart** — unleash one of your Demon Hearts’ special powers (unlimited uses)

The battle screen also shows your current XP toward the next level and a Bestiary strip listing every enemy type you’ve defeated and how many times.

## After the Game

Once all five Demon Hearts have been collected, the World Map displays: “Celebrate! All 5 demons have fallen. Keep fighting enemies and gain XP, but you can’t fight the demons.” Regular enemies remain fightable for continued leveling and gold.

-----

## What’s New From the Original Realm of Regions

- All new enemies across all five regions, plus a few returning favorites
- A fifth region (Shadow) and fifth boss (Shadow Demon) not in the original
- Tougher bosses overall — each Demon hits harder than the original Wizards
- New item shop with Steel Sword, Enchanted Sword, Magic Books, Pancakes, Dragon Armor, Enchanted Armor, and Bombs
- Demon Hearts with unique, reusable battle powers
- Bestiary tracker in the battle screen
- XP display in the battle screen
- Autosave with no manual save button
- No Inn — Demon Hearts (especially Wild Growth) cover healing instead
- No secret bosses, and defeated bosses cannot be re-fought
